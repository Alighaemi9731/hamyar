## Why these are pictures and not code any more

They were real markup under `/design/landing/{a,b,c}`, registered local-only and labelled
disposable — "the chosen one is rebuilt properly in 16.3 and these come out with it". 16.3
rebuilt it, so they came out.

But ADR 0021 also said, in its consequences, that A and B "are not lost work: they are
recorded at `/design/landing/{a,b}`". That made the routes the record, and deleting a
record is not a cleanup. So they were photographed first and the ADR now points here.

They were also the last thing in the repository still drawing the retired symbol beside
«همیار» set as text. Every shipping surface had moved to the commissioned wordmark by then
(#156, #159); these three had not, and could not, because they are a snapshot of a decision
already taken.

## What the reasoning was

`docs/walks/v2/gate-16.2.md` — the decision page as it was put to the owner, with what each
direction claimed and what it risked. ADR 0021 records what was chosen and why.
